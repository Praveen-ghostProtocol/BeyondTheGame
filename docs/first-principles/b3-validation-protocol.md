# B3 — Validation and Measurement Plan

This protocol tests the transformers' **design hypotheses**. It is a future plan, not an assertion that field studies have happened.

## Gate 1: Inventory census and reconciliation

1. With hostel permission, list a defined durable-asset sample; assign temporary anonymous IDs where needed.
2. At a fixed timestamp record actual physical location, responsible custodian/group, and verified condition (or unknown), alongside evidence quality.
3. Independently retrieve corresponding *existing records* without inferring missing item details from key custody.
4. Count complete-and-correct triads; complete-and-assessable triads; contradictions and missing attributes separately.
5. Report numerators/denominators, stock statuses and items that couldn't be verified; identify likely sampling bias.

## Gate 2: Direct event-boundary observation

Consent-based test of ordinary borrowing, multiple players sharing, off-ground custody transfer, return and tournament kit pickup. Log whether each physical event produces a record, and how much time it takes for the student, guard and secretary. No personal identifiers in committed datasets.

## Gate 3: Alternative controls (Phase C)

Test physical tags/kit cards, simplified QR flow, and full lifecycle flow with comparable tasks. Randomize/counterbalance order where practical, with first-time users. Measure (a) triad verification accuracy, (b) completion compliance, (c) elapsed issue/return time, (d) staff and student active effort, and (e) subjective usability. Include network outage and unregistered handover scenarios.

## Gate 4: Live pilot and independent audit (Phase E)

Use permissions and privacy-safe audit snapshots before/after the workflow change; compare matched contexts and avoid attributing differences to technology without considering sport-use mix, staff and season. Count unresolved transfers, stale records, quarantined stock, and repair reactivation.

## Success measurement

- **Primary:** correct location + accountable custodian + condition / audited durable assets.
- **Companion 1:** fully assessable triads / audited durable assets.
- **Companion 2:** correct triads / fully assessable triads; undefined when none assessable.
- **Safeguards:** guard time, student interaction time, share of skipped logs, 24/7 availability, tournament delay, privacy, cost.
- Explicitly date audits; avoid comparing unlike snapshots and category mixes.

## Evidence status as of B3

**No field audit or measured trial is complete.** The exported CSVs are hypothetical scenarios only. No observed effect size, equipment condition census, missed-loan frequency, or verified repair-case breakdown is available.
