# Evidence and privacy protocol

## Evidence categories
- **R — Reported:** direct statement by an identified role or informant; not necessarily independently observed.
- **O — Observed/measured:** directly witnessed, recorded or measured with method/date, potentially subject to sample limitations.
- **D — Documented:** supported by a relevant document, photograph, or record (with privacy controls).
- **I — Inferred:** interpretation or causal hypothesis based on evidence; must label uncertainty.
- **E — Estimated:** guesstimate/model assumption; clearly separate from baseline data.
- **S — Simulated:** deliberately synthetic trial data; never represented as field evidence.

## Research safeguards
1. Never convert a guesstimate or plausible example into an observed incident.
2. Preserve provenance of every numerical claim; record sample size and method for measurements.
3. Obtain permission for in-person observations, recordings, photographed registers and live pilots.
4. The repository is public. Do not commit names, roll numbers, room numbers, ID-card images, signatures, identifiable transaction history, private phone numbers, or unredacted purchase information.
5. Publish anonymized aggregates and non-identifying equipment images only where permitted.
6. AI-assisted drafting and analytical calculations should be disclosed in the final appendix per course brief and reviewed against evidence.
7. The current evidence does **not** establish causality between paper registration and equipment damage, nor does it establish the effectiveness of a proposed app.
