# C4 — One consolidated owner-decision batch (historical; all answered)

The project owner explicitly makes important decisions; these requests are grouped so no repeated piecemeal questionnaire is necessary.

1. **Study setting/permission:** Can we run a consented, staged trial with a small borrowed/owned set of safe sports-equipment props, and later obtain Alaknanda hostel permission for a real pilot? Prefer `STAGED FIRST` unless specifically authorized.
2. **Participants and checker:** May we recruit ~6 anonymous student/group participants for each scenario and one or more *independent* physical checkers? Should actual guards be asked to participate or should their role be enacted by a consenting volunteer? Default: no new guard duties.
3. **Physical props and equipment IDs:** Approve durable item IDs for rackets/bats/pads and quantity-only logs for shuttlecocks/other consumables. May we use removable labels on permitted items? Default: reversible tags only.
4. **Custody policy:** May a captain hold accountability for a full kit while on-field swaps within their group stay unlogged, with off-field transfers and return exceptions recorded? Default: team-level on-field responsibility; no assumption of fault for damage.
5. **Condition checks:** Who may verify usable/damaged state: sports secretary, designated team captain, consenting custodian, or another person? Default: designated responsible checker, **not the guard**.
6. **Time/burden limit:** Maximum acceptable *additional* time for a tournament kit handover? Suggested initial test ceiling **60 seconds**, provisional and not a measured result; normal borrowing should not be blocked by secretary absence.
7. **Selection criteria:** Approve or edit proposed weights: accountability 30%, burden 20%, reliability 15%, tournament usability 10%, lifecycle 10%, cost 10%, privacy/adoption 5%. Non-negotiable safety/privacy gates take precedence. Default: weights **unapproved** until explicit reply.
8. **Cost/privacy:** Any limit on prototype equipment/printing/software spending? May all participant and audit records use pseudonymous codes in a restricted workspace and only anonymous aggregates appear in the public repository? Default: no public identifying information; budget undecided.

These choices are required before actual participant trials and final selection, but **not** to generate the C4 protocol or code-level checks.
