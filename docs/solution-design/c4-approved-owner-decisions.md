## Boundaries
- Do not present owner approval as participant consent or as a substitute for hostel/guard operational authorization if duties or normal borrowing are changed.
- Guard remains key/register handler; guards are **not** asked to inspect item conditions by default.
- No labels or demo setup may damage equipment. Temporary handwritten inventory IDs or a local item-to-code list are acceptable if free reversible labels are unavailable.
- Do not record or publish student names, roll numbers, ID-card images, personal photos, or raw borrower logs in this **public** GitHub repository.
- Run the existing 12-scenario, 3-alternative, six-block plan only with real consent and independent checking. **216 is the planned upper schedule, not 216 observed trials.** Run a small preflight first to confirm practicality, then follow the agreed protocol or seek explicit approval for revisions.
- Measurement results, gate outcomes, and concept selection remain **NOT MEASURED / UNTESTED / NO SELECTION** until completed trials and owner review.
- Zero out-of-pocket spending does not imply zero labour, opportunity cost or long-term system operating cost: distinguish these in the final analysis.
