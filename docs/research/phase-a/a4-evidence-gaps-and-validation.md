## Specific unresolved decisions
1. **Physical-trial authority:** user has not approved changes to the guard's workflow or confirmed permission on behalf of hostel office. Obtain approval before any real trial.
2. **Reporting unit:** individual durable item, kit, or consumable stock lot; model each separately.
3. **What counts as custodian:** accountable student/team for items in use, hostel/guard as responsible entity for items in storage; finalize before auditing.
4. **Accountability validation method:** an accurate current record must be compared to independent observation at a matching time, with unrecorded attributes separately flagged as unavailable.
5. **Measurement scope:** proposed ordinary loan, kit, maintenance and procurement strata are not yet formally approved as one physical measurement exercise.

## Data provenance rules
- R: respondent-reported process or remembered incident.
- E: informal estimate recalled by respondent, unmeasured.
- S: explicit modeling assumption or hypothetical simulation; never present as observed evidence.
- I: analyst inference/hypothesis, requiring evidence.
- D: approved project decision.
- U: unknown/unmeasured.
- M (future): independently measured / audited with procedure, sample, time and source.

## Project and participant protection
No student identities, signatures, photos of ID cards, contact details, or unredacted register scans in the public repository. Record consent and avoid public accusations of causing specific damage. If private identifiable evidence is needed at all, hold securely outside the public project files.
