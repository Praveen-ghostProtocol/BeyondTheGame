## Causal hypotheses to investigate, not assert
- Missing event capture -> ambiguous asset custodian -> disputes and prolonged reconciliation.
- No inspection at return -> latent damage remains available in records -> delayed repair and usability uncertainty.
- No standardized kit-level check-out -> tournament convenience but incomplete custody history.
- No verified current stock/condition list -> avoidable inspection effort before purchases or potentially incorrect replacement decisions.

## Counterevidence / constraints
- Users generally find equipment easily and complaints are limited, so do not portray retrieval as an established major bottleneck.
- An additional mandatory form could obstruct pre-match preparation; capture data with minimal steps.
- 24/7 operation means the solution cannot depend on secretary always being present.
- The budget prioritization of medal-winning sports is a reported office preference, not a formal policy finding.
