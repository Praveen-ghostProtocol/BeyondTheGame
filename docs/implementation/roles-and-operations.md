# Roles and operating responsibilities

All accounts must be issued by an administrator. The classroom demo has a generated local password; real users need individual private credentials and consent. Codes visible in operational views are account codes, not ID-card images. Institutional operating rules remain authoritative.

| Role | Permitted operations | Restricted responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | Browse redacted catalogue; own requests/pickups/returns; offer/accept transfers; report damage; reconciliation case | No equipment registration, inspections, retirement, finance or other residents' borrowings |
| Captain | Student operations plus team-kit requests, delegated checking/inspection and audits | Cannot receive/inspect own borrowing or self-verify inventory they last operated; no financial authorization |
| Checker | Condition inspections, physical receipts, audits; personal ordinary borrowing | No inventory administration, retirement or procurement approval; same independence requirements |
| Guard | Key sign-out/return and reports of discovered damage | No equipment inspection, kit issue, retirement or finance; key return is not item verification |
| Maintenance | Work-order visibility and authorized vendor/repair/receipt milestones | Cannot authorize the initial repair, independently restore usable state or approve procurement |
| Sports secretary | Inventory/consumables/kits, requests, receipts/inspection, damage acknowledgement, repair authorization, retirement, procurement needs/quotes/receipts, reconciliation, event review | Cannot approve office finance or change confirmed office policy; cannot create or elevate accounts |
| Office | Procurement visibility, confirmed policy, explicit approval/payment evidence, aggregate export | No routine equipment custody, inventory registration or repair closure |
| Administrator | Provision/revoke accounts and full operational superuser access | Cannot accept a transfer for someone else, receive own borrowing or claim an independent audit of their own last-operated asset |

## Ordinary resident

1. Search or scan an equipment ID. Read state, condition and last recorded time.
2. Request the item and return time. Routine individual requests can be pre-authorized by the configured 24/7 policy. This is permission only.
3. Confirm pickup when physically holding the manifest. No secretary presence is needed for routine self-pickup, which is labelled a borrower declaration.
4. Share within the playing group under its responsible entity. If selected equipment leaves that group, offer custody to the receiving account, then have that resident accept actual receipt. Until acceptance, the original responsibility remains.
5. Declare return. A separate authorized checker receives actual components; unreceived equipment remains outstanding. Condition inspection is a separate step.

## Captain and tournament kit

Request and approve the kit before match day. At collection, confirm the existing manifest in one operation; the backend rejects the whole checkout if any durable component or consumable quantity is unavailable. Check physical contents proportionately. No per-touch on-field scans are needed. Return only actual components; a missing component cannot be hidden by returning its bag or the room key. The additional effort target of 60 seconds is still unmeasured.

## Damage, vendor repair and procurement

Report the discovery, including an unknown-ID report where necessary. Keep occurrence/fault unknown when unsupported. Reporting damage preserves actual custodian/location. Receive outstanding borrowed equipment before a repair case is opened. Record an authorized repair decision before moving to a vendor; after repair, record receipt and then inspect. If it still fails, reassess or cancel the in-store work order and consider authorized retirement.

Secretary records purchasing need and priority rationale. Office confirms the reported threshold policy, then records actual approval and payment/reimbursement evidence. Secretary physically receives each actual quantity and supplies new permanent durable IDs or a stock-lot reference. Partial delivery remains partial; an invoice never creates available inventory.

## Interruptions and evidence

If a request times out, first refresh. Idempotency protects replayed commands; do not create a second physical handover blindly. Record unresolved actual movement through Help & recovery. Secretary reconciles the real equipment, uses normal receipt/inspection or authorized correction as appropriate, and links the resolution. No automated synchronization or fines substitute for this process.

Independent physical audits need actual people and real references. Demo observations are synthetic forever. The application does not turn normal operator entries into field proof. Use the existing C4 workbook and consented study procedures for participant timing and comparative evaluation.
